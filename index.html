The problem

Jira configuration builds up quietly. Projects, schemes, workflows and fields, layered over years and spread across admin screens made for editing, not auditing.

So the hard questions stay hard. Which fields are still used? Which automations point at something that's gone? Who can delete issues in a sensitive project? What breaks if this status is renamed? AuditBuddy puts those answers in one place, for an admin to review before anything changes.

Sixteen categories. One inventory.

Fetch All loads every category into summary tiles and per-category tables. It's a read-only view of the instance, limited to what the connected account can read, and it's the data every auditor builds on.

Four auditors on top of the inventory.

Each one answers a question admins ask before a change, a cleanup or an access review, and leaves every result for a person to decide on.

01 · Impact Analyzer

What could break if I change or delete this?

Pick a custom field, automation rule, status, workflow or project. AuditBuddy builds a tree of the related items it finds in your loaded data, and an AI model rates each one Critical, High, Medium or Low with its reasoning and a recommendation. You get a structured first look before you make the change in Jira.

  • Affected groups and usersThe groups with access to the affected projects, their member counts and an estimated number of users, so you know who to tell.
  • Batch analysis PremiumReview up to 10 elements of any type in one tree, for larger restructures.
  • AI assistant chatAsk about the analysis in plain language, list matching issues or start a new analysis. Suggested questions on Free; free-form questions on Premium.

Good to knowRisk levels are AI judgments and can vary between runs. An empty tree is not proof of no impact.

02 · Field Auditor

Which of our custom fields are actually used?

Every field with its type, scope, screens, issues with data and last use. A cleanup filter narrows it to fields with no data that sit on no scanned screen. Instead of six hundred fields, you get a short list to review, with the screens and projects each one touches.

  • Project footprintCoverage for every field holding data in a project: high (80%+), medium (40–79%) or low (under 40%). A factual starting point for scoping a migration.
  • AI JQL generator PremiumTurn a plain-language question into JQL with an explanation, then copy it into Jira's issue search.

Good to know"Unused" means worth reviewing, not safe to delete. Free scans one project; Premium scans the whole instance.

03 · Automation Rules Auditor

Which automations point at things that no longer exist?

Every rule in one searchable list, including disabled rules and rules that touch fields. The Dead Reference Detector compares what rules reference with the fields, statuses and projects you've loaded, and points you to the rules that may fail after something was changed or removed.

  • Impact searchSearch a field ID, status name or project key to see every loaded rule that references it, before you rename or archive it.
  • Multi-entity report and Explain with AI PremiumPick several fields, statuses and projects at once and see which rules tie two or more of them together, with an AI summary grouped by finding.

Good to knowA match is a candidate for review, not proof of failure. Free shows how many rules are affected; Premium lists each one with what's missing and an Edit in Jira link.

04 · Access Auditor

See who can do what, across every project.

Trace access through groups, users and projects, with permissions marked HIGH, MED or SAFE. Large groups that hold high-risk permissions like Delete Issues or Administer Projects stand out, so access reviews, role changes and offboarding start from a clear picture.

  • Group accessOne group's members and every project it reaches, with roles and permissions. Compare two look-alike groups side by side.
  • User accessTrace one person's access through direct grants, groups and roles, or check a new hire against a colleague.
  • Project accessEvery role, group, user and permission for one project, for owners and auditors of sensitive work.

Good to knowCovers groups, roles and permission schemes; not global permissions, issue security or application access. HIGH means review. Needs Administer Projects.

How it reports

Built to flag, not to decide.

01

Candidates, not verdicts

Unused fields, dead references and high-risk permissions are flagged for an admin to review. Nothing is labelled safe to delete.

02

Only what's loaded

Analysis covers the data you've loaded and what the connected account can read. An empty result is not proof of no impact.

03

AI is labelled as AI

Risk levels, JQL and summaries come from an AI model and can be wrong. Check them in Jira. AI features need your data consent.

Getting started guide

Your first audit, step by step.

From creating your account to opening your first audit tool. Five steps, on the screens you'll see.

    • Go to audit.movonte.com.
    • Click Get started in the top bar, or Create one on the Log in form.
    • Choose the plan that fits you.
    • Enter your name, email and company. Phone is optional.
    • Create a password with at least 8 characters, one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter and one symbol, or click Generate secure password.
    • Click Create account.
    • Open the verification link we send you to activate your account.
    • Once you open it, you're signed in automatically.
    • Didn't get it? Check your spam folder or click Resend email.
    • Enter your Jira instance URL (for example, company.atlassian.net), the email of your Jira user and your API token.
    • No token yet? Generate one in your Atlassian Account Settings.
    • Credentials are used only for the current session and are never stored.
    • Click Connect to Jira and you're ready to audit.
    • The side panel on the left lists every tool available for the connected instance.
    • Pick one to start auditing.
